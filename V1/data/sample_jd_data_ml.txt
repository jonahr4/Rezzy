Machine Learning Engineer, Intern

Lumina AI is an applied ML company building recommendation and personalization systems for e-commerce and media platforms. Our models run inference on over 200M users daily. We're a team of 60, backed by Tier 1 VCs, and growing fast.

Role Overview:
We're looking for a Machine Learning Engineering intern to work on our model training infrastructure, feature engineering pipelines, and evaluation frameworks. This is a high-ownership role — you'll ship real changes to systems that affect production model quality.

- Write clear documentation and present findings to cross-functional stakeholders

- Experience training and evaluating ML models (classification, regression, or ranking)
- Familiarity with ML experiment tracking tools (MLflow, Weights & Biases, or similar)
- Understanding of feature engineering and data preprocessing best practices
- Comfortable working with large datasets

Nice to Have:
- Experience with deep learning frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX)
- Exposure to recommendation systems, collaborative filtering, or LLMs
- Familiarity with distributed data processing (Spark, Dask, Ray)
- Experience with cloud ML platforms (AWS SageMaker, GCP Vertex AI)
- Prior research experience or published work (even workshop papers count)

What We're Looking For:
- Intellectual curiosity about how ML systems work end-to-end
- Strong analytical mindset — you reason carefully about metrics and experimental validity
- A good communicator who can explain model behavior to non-technical stakeholders
- Self-starter who thrives in a fast-paced, research-adjacent environment
